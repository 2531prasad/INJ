# INJ — Phone-First Arcade Fighter

## Current build · v0.23 · 23 October 2026

A personal fighting game made for a friend. Phones are the primary platform; PC keyboard play remains available. Keep INJ cool, movement intuitive, and attacks responsive. INJ has escalating hit combos, a meter-powered Nitro Kick and aerial Shadow tosses. Level three introduces Goldline: a cute turquoise Audi R8 with gold rims and a second, humanoid machine form. Round four adds Blue Velvet, a theatrical blue plush opponent with a surprise Doraemon shrink-ray assist. INJ can teleport behind opponents with Blink. Four generated stages now match the opponents; existing fighter sprites are preserved.

For code changes, start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) and the [source map](docs/architecture.md). `index.html` now owns markup and ordered resource links. `js/` owns game systems, `styles/` owns presentation, and `audio.js` remains the procedural sound engine. There is no build step or production package dependency.

## Play

Open [index.html](index.html) directly, or run:

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`. Local artwork, scripts and synthesized audio work without asset downloads; Google Fonts are optional. Audio starts after entering the arena.

On iPhone, Safari Share → Add to Home Screen → enable Open as Web App if shown → Add, then launch INJ from its icon to use the app window without Safari's URL/tab bars. Ordinary Safari tabs may keep those bars. Physical Safari behavior remains unverified.

## Current behavior

- **Phone controls:** independent captured pointers for each thumb. The left joystick moves/crouches; its upward 90° wedge from 10:30 to 1:30 jumps, including diagonal jumps. Shallow upward tilts outside that wedge stay grounded. Touch-down starts eligible attacks immediately; busy fighters retain a 220 ms buffer. Larger round Punch/Kick buttons sit below a smaller icon-and-label row for Shadow, Upper and Guard, with Blink below at a fixed position. Nitro uses a fixed slot that appears only at full meter and hides immediately when spent; other buttons never move. Every touch target remains at least 44 px.
- **Display:** dynamic aspect ratio with an 18:9 desktop/portrait default and landscape fit. World height stays 225; width changes without stretching fighters or changing combat scale. Fighters occupy about half the arena height. Resize preserves health, attacks and spacing.
- **Quiet menu:** one borderless three-dot trigger at the arena's bottom edge. The two-row panel contains Resume, Full screen, Restart game, Profile, Sound and Music. Restart immediately returns to round one with full health and cleared combat state, preserving fullscreen and audio settings. No hand/focus halos or pulsing utility buttons.
- **Immersive play:** native fullscreen, expanded view and Home Screen app mode suppress browser double-tap/pinch gestures, callouts and page movement while preserving gameplay multitouch. Long dialogs can scroll vertically. Browser gestures return after leaving immersive play. iOS-owned system gestures cannot be removed by the game.
- **Rounds:** round one is gentle. Round two is the awakened Dummy, targeting roughly **60% player losses**. It favors evasions, one- or two-hit bursts and punishable pauses: 750–1150 ms after bursts, with an initial 350 ms opening; dodges expose a 300 ms opening. Damage, armor, pursuit and reactions are softer than the earlier 85% build. Outcomes are resolved by combat, never selected from a probability.
- **Victory:** an INJ win gets a 1.1-second knockout beat, then a dedicated arena celebration with her authored victory pose, a restrained close-up, gold flecks, a short fanfare, and the defeated opponent. The scene shows actual remaining health and the round’s best hit chain. It waits for **Next round** or Enter/Space. Combat, the timer and damage stop; pause freezes the celebration and Restart returns to round one. Only defeating the robot completes the Goldline encounter; the car still transforms. Losses keep MiMi’s dialogue, then retry the same level with fresh health and resources. Only wins advance. Losing to the robot retries Goldline from its car; losing to tiny Teddy retries full-size Teddy.
- **Level three / Goldline:** the cute turquoise Audi R8 has **125 car HP**, then **140 robot HP**, with only the current life visible. The robot reserve and phase labels stay hidden until the transformation; the new gold health bar and a fresh 99-second timer appear when the robot is revealed. The low car uses Goldline Dash, Thruster Hop, Gold Rim Disc and a temporary Rear-Up Strike. Crouching punches/sweeps reach it; high jabs can miss, and Shadow automatically angles downward. Its tuning target is **65% player losses**. After the car bar empties, a protected 2.1-second transformation starts the **80% player-loss target** robot phase: Piston Cross, Axle Breaker, Jet Uppercut, Orbital Slam and Boosted Cross, plus wheel-shield counters. Committed engine attacks retain light-hit poise after 60 ms; sweeps and charged Shadow can still stagger them outside a visible shield-counter windup. When the robot is revealed, INJ refills to full health and keeps Shadow charge and Nitro meter, including resources earned by the final car hit. The hit combo clears at the phase change. Each phase has a fresh 99-second clock; timing out loses to the boss. The car takes 85% of incoming damage; the robot now also takes 85% (up from 75%) and deals 130% of base damage (down from 135%). Its burst pauses last 480–780 ms. Visible shield beats vary, and after being staggered it briefly reassesses before attacking. The robot reads repeated attacks, punishes recovery, chooses anti-air strikes and low-attack counters, and retains punishable pauses. Winning Goldline advances to Blue Velvet in round four; later rounds repeat the teddy encounter. Restart always returns to round one. See [current balance notes](artifacts/r8-balance.md) for scripted results and limits.
- **Blue Velvet / round four onward:** a blue plush monster with long ears, huge blue eyes, a tan face and little teeth, using sixteen authored poses plus a corrected left-leg walking sprite. The walking pair alternates the far left leg and near right leg, with matching torso roots and grounded feet; the original right-leg pose is unchanged. Its neutral sprite is **10% taller than INJ** (123.2 vs 112 world units). One 140 HP pool, no bonus armor. After losing at least 33% HP, a single randomly delayed Doraemon cameo fires a shrink ray: the teddy becomes **66% smaller** (34% of its original height), keeping remaining HP. The protected 2.35-second sequence preserves INJ health/resources and the fight clock; pause freezes it. Curtain Call, Bow to Me, Drama Bomb and Velvet Verdict give way to Tiny Temper’s three low punches, Pocket Rocket, Not Your Toy and Mic Drop. The small form pursues faster and learns repeated crouched strikes, including attacks seen while knocked down. It hops over sweeps, mixes guards and hops against slides, escapes corners, and counters after defending. After a punching burst it can hop away from a learned low-kick follow-up. Its leaps rise early during their visible windup. High/aerial attacks and Shadow can catch these airborne responses. Varied attacks reduce its low-pattern memory, which expires after four seconds. Bursts retain punishable pauses, and ordinary hits still deal full damage; HP and armor are unchanged. Low strikes and Shadow reach it. The cameo cannot repeat within an encounter. A lethal hit before it starts still wins.
- **Blink / teleport:** BLINK or **E** disappears and reappears INJ behind the current opponent, facing them. Works standing, crouching and midair when free to act; cannot cancel an attack, hitstun or knockdown. The 460 ms sequence uses a crisp silhouette wipe and phase fragments, relocating at 190 ms. Landing is grounded and stays within arena bounds; corner side-switches gently separate the opponent inward to create rear space. It has a **10-second cooldown** in active fight time, visibly counted on the button and HUD. Pause, hit-stop and cutscenes freeze the cooldown; restart/next round reset it. Reduced motion keeps the disappearance/reappearance cue without moving fragments.
- **Combat:** punch chains, crouching strikes/sweeps, anti-air uppercuts, flying kicks, Shadow projectiles, guard/chip damage, knockdown and recovery. Three clean melee hits charge the next Shadow toss for an enhanced hit and knockdown. The first clean hit deals normal damage; following clean hits within 1.4 seconds deal ×1.1, ×1.2, ×1.3 and onward, shown as text over the arena with a transparent background. Damage is rounded to whole HP, then opponent armor applies. Taking a hit, being blocked, missing a melee attack, a phase change or expiry clears the chain. Down + forward + Kick adds a slide; down + Kick retains the sweep. Five clean hits fill the independent 100-point Nitro bar (20 per clean hit; taking an unblocked hit adds 6). NITRO / Q spends a full bar on a forward Nitro Kick from standing, crouching or midair; the crouching version strikes low. Its phase-style animation coils briefly, snaps to the authored kick, turns INJ neon green with short green phase silhouettes and directional flame ribbons, then recoils without sprite crossfades. Startup is 60 ms, active travel is 160 ms and recovery is 260 ms; a 660-unit/s burst keeps the original 105.6-unit travel budget. Reduced motion keeps the green sprite cue while suppressing echoes, flames and streaks. The recolor is cached at runtime; source image pixels and alpha are preserved. Shadow can also be thrown in the air and aims toward the opponent at release. Existing painted poses are reused, with no image edits.
- **Fighters:** authored transparent atlases with adult proportions and restrained painted materials. INJ/Dummy walking alternates four travel-driven poses; Blue Velvet uses two opposite-leg steps. Walking reverses when backing up and stops at walls. Sprite changes are immediate with no crossfades; attack anticipation is fast and root recovery is softer. INJ retains the hood, pale theatrical makeup, crimson lips and physical gold ring. Goldline has a separate sixteen-pose car/robot atlas with turquoise enamel, gold wheels and rounder car proportions. The fierce attack poses remain; [current edit prompts and provenance](assets/r8-generation-v2.md).
- **Stages:** each encounter has a new detailed generated backdrop: Dummy’s warm **Cedar Courtyard**, Awakened Dummy’s **Storm Sanctum**, Goldline’s turquoise-and-gold **Foundry**, and Blue Velvet’s **Toybox Opera**. Goldline keeps its foundry across transformation; full-size and tiny Teddy share the theatre. Later Teddy rounds reuse that opponent’s stage. Quiet themed lights/motes share the arena camera; moving decoration stops under reduced motion. The distant cosmetic awakened duel appears only on the sanctum bridge. Stages never change combat randomness. [Source art and prompts](assets/stage-generation.md).
- **MiMi:** losses lead to a tap-paced five-line exchange between INJ and her own cat. The cat gets the final roast. One exchange includes “MiMi, please stop.” The portrait shares INJ's CRT treatment. Next advances, Fight Again retries the current level, and Skip/Escape bypasses the exchange into that same retry. Wins open the victory scene instead.
- **Audio/lifecycle:** original continuous 112 BPM, 16-bar procedural arcade score; coordinated synthesized combat cues, including engine, thruster, rim and transformation sounds; independent persisted sound/music controls. Initial profile, pause, loss dialogue, focus loss and backgrounding stop combat and clear held input. Hidden pages suspend audio. Menu music continues quietly; reduced-motion settings suppress decoration.

## Controls

| Action | Phone | Keyboard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Move / crouch | Joystick left, right / down | A/D/S or arrows |
| Jump | Joystick 10:30–1:30 wedge | W, up arrow or Space |
| Punch | Punch | J |
| Kick / sweep / flying kick | Kick; down or jump changes move | K |
| Uppercut | Upper | U |
| Shadow / charged Shadow, ground or air | Shadow | L |
| Slide | Down + forward + Kick | Down + forward + K |
| Nitro Kick, any stance, full bar | Nitro (appears when ready) | Q |
| Teleport behind opponent | Blink · countdown while cooling | E |
| Guard | Hold Guard | Shift or I |
| Pause / resume | Three-dot menu / Resume | Escape |
| Mute | Menu → Sound | M |
| Restart / fullscreen | Three-dot menu | Same menu actions |

## Verification

Run `node tests/structure.cjs`, then `node tests/browser.cjs`. Playwright and Chrome/Chromium are required only for browser checks; `INJ_PLAYWRIGHT` and `INJ_CHROME` override tool paths. The runner owns its server/browser, intercepts only a test copy of `js/main.js`, and separately exercises untouched HTTP and direct-file startup.

The latest suite passes **381 Chrome checks** across phone and desktop sizes, including genuine simultaneous touch, gestures, jump boundaries, menu/restart/fullscreen flows, animation, combat, opponent behavior, MiMi dialogue and audio. [validation.json](artifacts/validation.json) records results and three 100-fight scripted benchmarks (Dummy, car and robot), plus forty full Teddy fights using four policies and twenty isolated tiny-phase repeated-kick fights. The focused crouched-Kick/slide policies win 0/20 trials against the adaptive defense; varied strategies still beat Teddy. Boss phases are tested separately with fresh INJ health, matching the new refill at robot reveal. The seeded robot benchmark records 21/100 INJ wins against seven basic policies. Teddy tests include Blink and Nitro; basic Dummy/R8 policies omit those abilities and aerial Shadow. Benchmarks use simple policies and do not exercise every move. These policies do not establish a human win rate.

The one-time [extraction comparison](artifacts/module-refactor.json) matched all 59 original function bodies and 64 initializers after normalizing cross-file references, plus DOM structure/attributes/text and 169 CSS rules in the same cascade. Width/frame time intentionally moved into shared objects, and startup is now explicit. Runtime checks supplement this source comparison.

Physical-phone ergonomics, real Safari/Home Screen chrome and interruptions, and subjective speaker/headphone listening remain unverified. The offline score render has finite audible samples, no clipped samples and no silent arrangement gaps; that is a signal check, not listening approval.
